Before your appointment

What you need
to know.

A few practical points from the practice's booking information.

Referral & funding

  • A GP referral is preferable, but not essential.
  • Most patients self-refer and self-fund.
  • Patients with private health insurance are also seen.
  • Recognition is stated for CIGNA, PruHealth and WPA.

Payment

  • Fees must be paid in advance to secure an appointment.
  • Flexible payment plans of 1 to 3 months are offered.
  • Payment can be made by BACS transfer.
  • Debit or credit card via PayPal, or cash, are also accepted.

Important attendance policy

  • Late attendance exceeding 15 minutes is recorded as non-attendance.
  • The appointment must then be rebooked.
