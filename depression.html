Recognise the picture

Depression can affect more than mood.

The supplied content emphasises that symptoms may be physical, psychological and social, so assessment and treatment should consider the whole person.

01 • Psychological

Sadness & hopelessness

Persistent low mood and feelings of hopelessness are central features described in the source.

02 • Behavioural

Loss of interest

Activities that were once enjoyable may lose their appeal, described as anhedonia.

03 • Physical

Sleep, appetite & energy

Changes in sleep or appetite and fatigue may occur.

04 • Cognitive

Concentration

Difficulty concentrating is included among the symptoms described.

Depression facts

Numbers with context.

The following figures are reproduced from the supplied source material and should be read in the context of the dates and references provided there.

~6%

Clinical criteria

The supplied material states that about 6% of the population meets clinical criteria for depression or dysthymia at any one time.

10–20%

Lifetime prevalence

The source states that overall lifetime prevalence appears to be 10–20%.

≥50%

Anxiety overlap

The supplied material states that at least 50% will also have anxiety problems.

Treatment & recovery

Effective treatment can be multidimensional.

The supplied material describes psychological therapy, medication and combinations of approaches. For moderate or severe depression, it states that NICE recommends antidepressant medication together with a high-intensity psychological intervention.

Approaches described in the source

Individual treatment should be determined with an appropriate healthcare professional.

Recovery is more than symptom reduction.

The supplied material describes effective and timely treatment as needing to address physical, psychological and social symptoms. Long-term recovery and relapse prevention are also highlighted.
