“With compassion, empathy, and a holistic biopsychosocial approach, you can recover too.”

Welcome to IamPsychiatry in Luton, where expert private mental healthcare has been provided since 2008. Led by Dr Iqbal Mohiuddin, a consultant psychiatrist on the GMC Specialist Register since 2013, the service specialises in personalised psychiatric assessments and treatments.

Services include ADHD assessments, diagnosis and medication management, tailored to the unique needs of each patient. Conveniently located in Luton, the practice is dedicated to compassionate care through a holistic approach, with effective and timely support.

You can be assessed, diagnosed and begin effective treatment within 4 weeks. The aim is to provide exceptional private healthcare through a collaborative treatment plan that aligns with your needs and values.

Patients have been supported from diverse backgrounds throughout London, St Albans, Hertfordshire, Bedfordshire, Luton, Milton Keynes and Buckinghamshire.

How it works

A straightforward route
to getting the right support.

The published pathways use pre-assessment information and a GP brief medical summary to support an efficient assessment.

01 / PRE-ASSESSMENT

Share your information

Complete the relevant pre-assessment form and provide the required GP brief medical summary.

02 / ASSESSMENT

Meet your clinician

Have a structured psychiatric or adult ADHD assessment through the appropriate pathway.

03 / CLARITY

Diagnosis & formulation

Where appropriate, receive diagnostic clarification and a personalised clinical treatment plan.

04 / TREATMENT

Move forward

Begin an agreed treatment pathway, including medication management or titration where indicated.
