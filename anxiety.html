When should you seek help?

Anxiety may warrant professional assessment when it becomes excessive, persistent or interferes with daily activities, or when symptoms are severe or chronic.

Self-help & support

Small steps can support recovery.

These strategies are drawn directly from the supplied material.

Things you can try

  • Talk about your feelings with a friend, family member, health professional or counsellor.
  • Use calming breathing exercises such as 7:11 diaphragmatic breathing.
  • Exercise — running, walking, swimming and yoga may help you relax.
  • Explore strategies for improving sleep if you are struggling to sleep.
  • Eat a healthy balanced diet with regular meals to keep energy levels stable.
  • Consider peer support, where people use their experiences to help each other.

NHS psychological therapies

The supplied material notes that free psychological therapies such as CBT may be available through the NHS, and that people can self-refer directly to an NHS psychological therapy service (IAPT / Wellbeing service) without a GP referral.
