Does childhood history matter?

What if you were never formally diagnosed as a child?

Tap to reveal answer ↻
Key point
✓

Childhood symptoms are important.

The supplied material states that, under current UK diagnostic guidance, Adult ADHD cannot be confirmed unless symptoms have been present from childhood. Old school reports or information from people who knew you as a child may help when history is difficult to recall.

Common misconception

“I wasn't diagnosed as a child, so I can't have ADHD.”

The supplied material distinguishes absence of a childhood diagnosis from absence of childhood symptoms. Childhood history may need to be explored during assessment.

Assessment principle

Childhood history can still be reconstructed.

Old school reports, parents, teachers or other people who knew you well as a child may provide useful information when memories are unclear.

Interactive check

Yes or no?

Choose an answer. The card will immediately give you the learning point.

ADHD can first appear only in adulthood.

The supplied material states that ADHD cannot develop for the first time in adults and that symptoms should have been present from childhood.

A person can have ADHD without taking medication.

The supplied material lists psychotherapy, life coaching and lifestyle changes as non-medication approaches.

A recent problem with no childhood history automatically indicates ADHD.

Consider the childhood-onset information provided in the guide.

Functional impact across life areas can be relevant to diagnosis.

The supplied material gives examples involving work or education, driving, friendships and relationships.

Assessment pathway

From suspicion to structured assessment.

A free online test may highlight symptoms and suitability; a private assessment then explores the clinical picture in greater depth.

What happens next?

According to the supplied material, Adult ADHD assessment combines information from questionnaires, clinical interview and behavioural assessment. Childhood symptoms and their impact across different areas of life are important parts of the assessment.
